# Security Policy

Thought Parameters takes the security of our software and our clients seriously. This policy applies to every public repository in the [ThoughtParameters](https://github.com/ThoughtParameters) organization unless a repository defines its own.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Report vulnerabilities privately through either channel:

- GitHub: use **"Report a vulnerability"** on the repository's **Security** tab (private vulnerability reporting)
- Email: **hello@thoughtparameters.com** with the subject line `SECURITY`

Please include the affected repository, version or commit, steps to reproduce, and the impact you observed.

## What to Expect

- We acknowledge new reports within **3 business days**.
- We send an initial assessment and planned next steps within **10 business days**.
- We coordinate a disclosure date with you and credit you in the advisory, unless you prefer to stay anonymous.

## Scope

In scope: code in public ThoughtParameters repositories. Out of scope: denial-of-service testing, social engineering, and third-party services we don't control.
