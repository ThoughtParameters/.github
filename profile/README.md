## 👋 Who We Are

Thought Parameters is a remote-first computer systems design firm based in Orange County, California. We are senior engineers who have run production platforms at **Netflix, Apple, and Workday** and in PCI-, HIPAA-, and SOX-regulated enterprises. We bring that experience to teams that need it now, without a six-month hiring search.

We don't hand off a slide deck and leave. We ship working software, automate the infrastructure under it, lock it down, and stay to **operate** it.

> [!TIP]
> **Free consultation:** call **1 (888) 875-1399**, email **[hello@thoughtparameters.com](mailto:hello@thoughtparameters.com)**, or message us on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/thoughtparameters/). Tell us what you're building, what's broken, or what you can't hire for fast enough.
